Privacy

Updated November 5, 2026.

WLNG is a VTuber stream discovery site. You can browse and play streams without signing in or earning XP.

Accounts and activity XP

Optional Google sign-in is handled by Google and Supabase. WLNG never receives your Google password. Supabase manages your account identity, including the profile information supplied during sign-in. Your session is saved in this browser so you can remain signed in.

If you start activity XP, Cloudflare D1 stores your Supabase user ID, saved XP, window timestamps, and temporary request identifiers used to prevent duplicate updates. XP is for the optional WLNG activity window and is independent of video playback. It has no cash value. A window can continue for up to 30 minutes after you close the page.

Activity totals are retained until you use “Delete my activity data” while signed in. This control is at the bottom of Settings in the Danger zone; hold the red unlock button for two seconds to reveal it. Temporary request records older than 48 hours are removed on your next XP update. Deleting activity data stops the window and removes your activity records; it does not delete your Supabase or Google account.

Optional public profiles

Choosing a WLNG username stores it with your verified account ID in Cloudflare D1. If Public profile is enabled, visitors with your link can see your username, level (including XP earned within your activity window), XP within that level, equipped XP-bar and badge appearance, and optional description. Your selected profile theme and optional agency logo are also visible. Site and profile themes can be saved independently. You may also display up to ten granted profile badges. Badge ownership and your selected badges are stored with your account in Cloudflare D1; grant and revoke changes follow your account across username changes. There is no profile directory or search. No Google email address or internal account ID is included in the public profile.

Usernames can change once a week, and descriptions every five minutes. Former usernames remain reserved to your account and redirect to your current name to protect existing links. You can hide your profile immediately with the Public profile setting. Activity-data deletion resets XP; it does not erase or rename your profile or erase your saved cosmetic preferences. Level-locked appearances stop applying when you no longer meet their requirements. Manage the description and visibility in Account settings.

Browser preferences

Theme, density, reduced motion, default agency, favorite creators, and your sign-in session are stored in this browser. Favorites and Settings preferences remain browser-local. Multiview stores your current grid and up to ten named layouts, including channel choices, tile positions, chat panels and zoom, in this browser. These layouts are not uploaded to your account. Equipped XP-bar skins, earned themes and badges, plus your optional public-profile color preference, are saved with your account in Cloudflare D1. A recent XP and equipped-appearance snapshot is also cached in this browser and shared between same-origin tabs to reduce repeat requests; it is reused briefly before a new page load checks the server again. The snapshot does not contain your authentication tokens. Clear site data in your browser to remove local preferences and session data, or sign out to end the local session.

Services used

GitHub Pages hosts the site. Cloudflare handles API requests and storage, and uses the connecting IP for approximate request limits. Provider infrastructure and enabled operational logging may process request metadata for security and reliability. WLNG does not intentionally log authentication tokens.

Stream information comes from Holodex. Playing a video or opening live chat loads YouTube, which may use cookies and process data under Google’s Privacy Policy. The site also loads fonts from Google Fonts and a locally bundled Supabase client library.

See the privacy information for Supabase, Cloudflare, GitHub. Project maintenance information is available in the WLNG repository.
