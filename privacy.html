Privacy

Updated November 4, 2026.

WLNG is a VTuber stream discovery site. You can browse and play streams without signing in or earning XP.

Accounts and activity XP

Optional Google sign-in is handled by Google and Supabase. WLNG never receives your Google password. Supabase manages your account identity, including the profile information supplied during sign-in. Your session is saved in this browser so you can remain signed in.

If you start activity XP, Cloudflare D1 stores your Supabase user ID, saved XP, window timestamps, and temporary request identifiers used to prevent duplicate updates. XP is for the optional WLNG activity window and is independent of video playback. It has no cash value. A window can continue for up to 30 minutes after you close the page.

Activity totals are retained until you use “Delete my activity data” while signed in. Temporary request records older than 48 hours are removed on your next XP update. Deleting activity data stops the window and removes your activity records; it does not delete your Supabase or Google account.

Browser preferences

Theme, density, reduced motion, default agency, favorite creators, and your sign-in session are stored in this browser. Favorites and display preferences currently do not synchronize through your account. Clear site data in your browser to remove local preferences and session data, or sign out to end the local session.

Services used

GitHub Pages hosts the site. Cloudflare handles API requests and storage, and uses the connecting IP for approximate request limits. Provider infrastructure and enabled operational logging may process request metadata for security and reliability. WLNG does not intentionally log authentication tokens.

Stream information comes from Holodex. Playing a video or opening live chat loads YouTube, which may use cookies and process data under Google’s Privacy Policy. The site also loads fonts from Google Fonts and the Supabase client library from jsDelivr.

See the privacy information for Supabase, Cloudflare, GitHub, and jsDelivr. Project maintenance information is available in the WLNG repository.
